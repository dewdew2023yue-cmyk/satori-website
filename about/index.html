01About

Owners, not vendors.

We take on only a handful of properties at a time, on purpose. Every recommendation is one we'd stake our own property on.

Satori is founder-led, by a principal who's spent her career in hospitality, working with the world's biggest travel and hotel brands on how they drive revenue. Satori exists to bring that playbook to the independent properties that deserve it most.

And the longer arc is ownership: we're building toward boutique properties of our own. Which means we advise like owners, not vendors.

Satori is a hospitality marketing agency for independent hotels that would rather be unmistakable than everywhere. We'd rather go deep with a few owners we believe in than spread ourselves thin, so we say yes only when the fit is obvious.

02Is this you?

We work best with

  • Independent, owner-run properties with real place-based character.
  • Ten to thirty rooms.
  • An average nightly rate of at least $350, with the intention of raising it over time to match the market and the positioning.
  • Owners who want to build something worth protecting.

We're not the fit for

  • Chains and management-company properties.
  • Discount-led strategies.
  • Anyone looking for a quick campaign instead of a real engine.

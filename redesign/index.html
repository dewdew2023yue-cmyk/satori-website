The gap

Your guests already love you. Your website, your feed and your booking engine should say so.

The best independent hotels have glowing reviews and a product people cross the province for. Then a guest finds them online, and the site is ten years old, the feed went quiet last summer, and the cheapest rate is on someone else's platform.

Satori closes that gap. We make the way you show up online as good as the stay itself, and we make sure the booking that follows comes to you directly, at the rate you deserve.

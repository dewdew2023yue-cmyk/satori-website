Questions

Hotel marketing, answered.

What does a hotel marketing agency do?

A hotel marketing agency runs the work that fills your rooms: pricing and rate strategy, the direct booking path, your website, social media, photography, paid ads and guest email. Satori does all of it for independent and boutique hotels, starting with the numbers so every piece of marketing is measured against your margin.

How can an independent hotel get more direct bookings?

Start with rate parity and a direct-booking advantage, so your own website is never more expensive than the booking platforms. Then make the booking path fast on a phone, keep your social feed and photography as good as the stay, and use remarketing and email to bring past guests back directly.

How much does hotel marketing cost?

Most partnerships start with the Satori Audit at $3,500, credited to your first month if we keep working together. Our monthly retainer covers the full marketing engine and costs about the same as a single in-house marketing hire once salary and benefits are counted.

Should a boutique hotel hire a marketing manager or an agency?

A marketing manager brings one set of skills and takes months to recruit and ramp up. An agency like Satori brings strategy, revenue, brand, web, social, photography and paid from the first month, for roughly the same budget, with no recruiting or turnover.
